I am an AI researcher advancing science and healthcare through innovative machine-learning methods. My interests span **geometric deep learning**, **large language models**, and **AI for biology/healthcare**.

Currently I work at [Sapient Intelligence](https://github.com/sapientinc/HRM), applying [Hierarchical Reasoning Models](https://github.com/sapientinc/HRM) to healthcare scenarios in collaboration with Peking Union Medical College Hospital. I hold an M.Phil. from the [University of Cambridge](https://www.cam.ac.uk/) (Distinction, ranked 8/60) and a B.Sc. from [UCL](https://www.ucl.ac.uk/) (First Class Honors).

I welcome opportunities to collaborate — feel free to reach out via email or LinkedIn. For more details, see my [About](/about/) page.
